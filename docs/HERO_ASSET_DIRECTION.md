### Document Version: 1.0 (Phase 04A Baseline)

---

## 1. Executive Asset Strategy

Essence Indonesia is positioned as a **Premium Indonesian Commodity Exporter** built upon two equal strategic pillars: **Vanilla (50%) and Coffee (50%)**.

The Hero visual must establish:
> **Indonesia & Equatorial Agricultural Terroir first, commodities second.**

We deliberately avoid forcing generic stock images of coffee or placing oversized product cutouts side-by-side in the hero viewport, which would degrade the experience into a generic commodity marketplace. Instead, the hero opens with deep atmospheric mystery, organic agricultural textures, and authentic Indonesian provenance.
